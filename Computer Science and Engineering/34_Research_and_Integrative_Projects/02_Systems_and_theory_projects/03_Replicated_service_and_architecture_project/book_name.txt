Primary book: Distributed Systems, 4th edition — Maarten van Steen and Andrew S. Tanenbaum.
Source: https://www.distributed-systems.net/index.php/books/ds4/
Source type: Official book site with free personal digital edition.
Read: Review communication, coordination, replication, and fault-tolerance themes needed by the design.
Study checklist:
01. State the system and failure model.
02. Define replicated-state invariants.
03. Separate safety and liveness evidence.
04. Inject delay, duplication, and crashes.
05. Measure workload and recovery behavior.
06. Document unhandled failure classes.
Completion: Build or simulate a replicated Rust service and provide a design document, invariant argument, failure experiment, and performance report.
