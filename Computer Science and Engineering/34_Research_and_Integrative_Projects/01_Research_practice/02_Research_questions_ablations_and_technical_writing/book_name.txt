Primary book: Software Engineering at Google — Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck, and Hyrum Wright, editors.

Read: Documentation, Knowledge Sharing, and measuring engineering outcomes themes.
Study checklist:
01. Turn an observation into a falsifiable question.
02. Choose a meaningful baseline.
03. Design an ablation that isolates a mechanism.
04. Quantify uncertainty and resource cost.
05. Preserve reproducible artifacts.
06. Distinguish evidence, interpretation, and speculation.
Completion: Write a small research report with a question, baseline, controlled experiment, counterexample, and reproducibility instructions.
