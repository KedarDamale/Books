Primary book: Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces — Remzi H. Arpaci-Dusseau and Andrea C. Arpaci-Dusseau.
Source: https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/
Source type: Author-provided open book.
Read: I/O Devices, Hard Disk Drives, RAID, and Flash-based SSDs themes.
Study checklist:
01. Explain device queues and interrupts.
02. Model disk seek and transfer costs.
03. Compare HDD and SSD behavior.
04. Calculate RAID capacity and failure exposure.
05. Distinguish redundancy and backup.
06. Explain write amplification.
Completion: Analyze storage traces and compare RAID layouts under stated capacity, failure, and rebuild assumptions.
