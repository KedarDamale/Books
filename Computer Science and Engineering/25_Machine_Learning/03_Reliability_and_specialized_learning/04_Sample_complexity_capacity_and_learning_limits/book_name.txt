Primary book: Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms — Shai Shalev-Shwartz and Shai Ben-David.
Source: https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~shais/UnderstandingMachineLearning/
Source type: Author-provided open textbook.
Read: A Gentle Start, A Formal Learning Model, Learning via Uniform Convergence, and The Bias-Complexity Tradeoff themes.
Study checklist:
01. Distinguish hypothesis class and fitted parameters.
02. Interpret a generalization bound.
03. Explain capacity and overfitting.
04. Relate sample size and error probability.
05. State distribution and independence assumptions.
06. Separate information-theoretic and computational limits.
Completion: Work through a finite-class generalization bound and compare its qualitative prediction with a synthetic learning experiment.
Extension book: Mathematics for Machine Learning — Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, and Cheng Soon Ong.
Extension focus (optional): Revisit derivations and the assumptions behind numerical learning methods.
Source: https://mml-book.github.io/
