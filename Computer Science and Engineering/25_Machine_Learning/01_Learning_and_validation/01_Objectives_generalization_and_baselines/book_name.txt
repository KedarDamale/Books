Primary book: An Introduction to Statistical Learning: with Applications in Python — Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jonathan Taylor.
Source: https://www.statlearning.com/
Source type: Author-provided open book.
Read: Statistical Learning themes: prediction, inference, loss, flexibility, bias, and variance.
Study checklist:
01. Define a target and prediction horizon.
02. Choose a loss matching consequences.
03. Explain empirical and expected risk.
04. Compare model flexibility and variance.
05. Establish a meaningful baseline.
06. Identify distribution-shift assumptions.
Completion: Formulate a supervised task and show whether a learned model beats a justified baseline under a clean evaluation split.
