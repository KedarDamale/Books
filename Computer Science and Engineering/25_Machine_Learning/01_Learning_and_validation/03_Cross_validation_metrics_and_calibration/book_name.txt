Primary book: scikit-learn User Guide — scikit-learn contributors.
Source: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html
Source type: Official online guide.

01. Choose grouped or time-aware splitting.

03. Relate thresholds to error costs.
04. Explain calibration and discrimination.
05. Use nested validation when selecting models.
06. Report uncertainty and subgroup performance.
