Primary book: Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models — Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, and Pieter Abbeel.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239
Source type: Authors' open research paper.

06. Identify discretization and evaluation limitations.
Completion: Train a small PyTorch denoiser on a toy distribution and compare generated samples and compute costs under several schedules.
