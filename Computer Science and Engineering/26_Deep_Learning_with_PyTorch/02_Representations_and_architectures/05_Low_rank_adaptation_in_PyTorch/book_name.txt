Primary book: LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models — Edward J. Hu, Yelong Shen, Phillip Wallis, Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, Yuanzhi Li, Shean Wang, Lu Wang, and Weizhu Chen.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685
Source type: Authors' open research paper.
Read: Paper themes: low-rank parameterization, frozen base weights, trainable parameter count, and empirical comparison.

02. Derive trainable parameter counts.
03. Initialize adapters without changing initial outputs.
04. Separate rank and update scaling.
05. Merge updates for inference.
06. Compare memory, quality, and baseline assumptions.
Completion: Implement a low-rank adapter directly in PyTorch and compare it with full tuning under the same small-data protocol.
