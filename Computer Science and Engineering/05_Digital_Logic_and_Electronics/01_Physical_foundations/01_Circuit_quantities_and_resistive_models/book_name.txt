Primary book: Foundations of Analog and Digital Electronic Circuits — Anant Agarwal and Jeffrey H. Lang.
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/pages/readings/
Source type: Book with MIT reading guide.
Read: Lumped abstraction, KVL, KCL, resistive networks, and nodal analysis themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define voltage, current, resistance, and power.
02. Apply Kirchhoff current and voltage laws.
03. Solve a small resistive network.
04. Explain ground as a reference.
05. Compare ideal and physical components.
06. State the lumped-model validity assumptions.
Completion: Solve and simulate a resistor network, checking units, conservation, and a model limitation.
