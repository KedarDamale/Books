Primary book: CMOS VLSI Design: A Circuits and Systems Perspective, 4th edition — Neil H. E. Weste and David Money Harris.
Source: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/cmos-vlsi-design-a-circuits-and-systems-perspective/P200000003427
Source type: Publisher book page and official companion.
Read: CMOS processing, circuit delay, interconnect, and combinational circuit design themes.
Study checklist:
01. Relate transistor networks and logic functions.
02. Explain basic layout design-rule constraints.
03. Estimate gate and interconnect delays.
04. Compare transistor sizing choices.
05. Analyze dynamic and leakage power.
06. Separate functional verification and timing closure.
Completion: Analyze a small CMOS logic path and explain how sizing or wire load changes delay, area, and power.
