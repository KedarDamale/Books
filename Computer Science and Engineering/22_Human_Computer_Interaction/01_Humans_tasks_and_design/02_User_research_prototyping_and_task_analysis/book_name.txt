Primary book: Human-Computer Interaction, 3rd edition — Alan Dix, Janet Finlay, Gregory D. Abowd, and Russell Beale.
Source: https://www.hcibook.com/e3/chapters/
Source type: Official book site and chapter outlines.
Read: Design Process, User Support, Task Analysis, and prototyping themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define a target user and context.
02. Observe a task without leading questions.
03. Separate evidence and designer assumptions.
04. Decompose task goals and actions.
05. Prototype competing interaction flows.
06. Record research and sampling limitations.
Completion: Create two low-fidelity prototypes for an observed task and explain the evidence behind their differences.
