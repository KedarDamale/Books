Primary book: Human-Computer Interaction, 3rd edition — Alan Dix, Janet Finlay, Gregory D. Abowd, and Russell Beale.
Source: https://www.hcibook.com/e3/chapters/
Source type: Official book site and chapter outlines.
Read: The Human, The Interaction, and Interaction Design Basics themes.
Study checklist:
01. Explain perception and working-memory constraints.
02. Distinguish system and user mental models.
03. Analyze gulf of execution and evaluation.
04. Connect affordances and feedback.
05. Identify error-prone interactions.
06. State limits of simplified user models.
Completion: Analyze a real interface using a concrete task and propose a redesign justified by observed interaction problems.
