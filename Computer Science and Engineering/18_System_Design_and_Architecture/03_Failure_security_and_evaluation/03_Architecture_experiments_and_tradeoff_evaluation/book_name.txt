Primary book: Software Architecture in Practice, 4th edition — Len Bass, Paul Clements, and Rick Kazman.
Source: https://www.informit.com/store/software-architecture-in-practice-9780136886099
Source type: Publisher book page and contents.
Read: Designing an Architecture, Analyzing an Architecture, and architecture evaluation themes.
Study checklist:
01. Identify architecturally significant uncertainty.
02. Compare alternatives against scenarios.
03. Build a focused prototype.
04. Record measured versus assumed behavior.
05. Analyze sensitivity and failure cases.
06. Explain a justified rejected alternative.
Completion: Evaluate two architectures for the same workload with prototypes, measurements, and explicit reasons for the final choice.
Extension book: Performance Modeling and Design of Computer Systems: Queueing Theory in Action — Mor Harchol-Balter.
Extension focus (optional): Deepen workload-dependent capacity and scheduling analysis.
Source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~harchol/PerformanceModeling/book.html
