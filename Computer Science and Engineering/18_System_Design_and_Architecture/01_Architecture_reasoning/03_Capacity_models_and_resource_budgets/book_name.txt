Primary book: Performance Modeling and Design of Computer Systems: Queueing Theory in Action — Mor Harchol-Balter.
Source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~harchol/PerformanceModeling/book.html
Source type: Author book site and detailed contents.
Read: Queueing terminology, Little's Law, operational laws, and bottleneck analysis themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define arrival rate and service demand.
02. Apply Little's Law with units.
03. Separate throughput and concurrency.
04. Identify utilization saturation.
05. Estimate storage and network demand.
06. State workload and stationarity assumptions.
Completion: Build a capacity model from a synthetic trace and vary assumptions to locate the first likely bottleneck.
