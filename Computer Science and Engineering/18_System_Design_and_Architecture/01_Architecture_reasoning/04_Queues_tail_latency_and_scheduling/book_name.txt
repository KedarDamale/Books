Primary book: Performance Modeling and Design of Computer Systems: Queueing Theory in Action — Mor Harchol-Balter.
Source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~harchol/PerformanceModeling/book.html
Source type: Author book site and detailed contents.
Read: Single-server queues, variability, scheduling policies, and server farms themes.
Study checklist:
01. Explain why variability increases waiting.
02. Derive a simple queue's mean response.
03. Compare mean and tail latency.
04. Analyze head-of-line blocking.
05. Compare pooling and partitioned queues.
06. Identify unstable arrival-service regimes.
Completion: Simulate queues with equal mean service time but different variability and explain the observed latency distributions.
