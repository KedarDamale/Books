Primary book: Mathematics for Computer Science — Eric Lehman, F. Thomson Leighton, and Albert R. Meyer.
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/pages/readings/
Source type: MIT open textbook.
Read: Counting, permutations, combinations, discrete probability, and expectation themes.
Study checklist:
01. Apply sum and product counting rules.
02. Count with and without replacement.
03. Use inclusion-exclusion.
04. Calculate conditional probabilities.
05. Apply linearity of expectation.
06. Separate independence from disjointness.
Completion: Derive collision probabilities for a small identifier space and compare the derivation with a Python simulation.
