Primary book: Mathematics for Computer Science — Eric Lehman, F. Thomson Leighton, and Albert R. Meyer.
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/pages/readings/
Source type: MIT open textbook.
Read: Induction, strong induction, recursive definitions, and state machines themes.
Study checklist:
01. Prove a base case and inductive step.
02. Choose weak or strong induction.
03. Prove structural properties recursively.
04. State a loop invariant.
05. Establish preservation and postconditions.
06. Prove termination with a decreasing measure.
Completion: Prove correctness and termination of binary search, explicitly handling an empty interval.
