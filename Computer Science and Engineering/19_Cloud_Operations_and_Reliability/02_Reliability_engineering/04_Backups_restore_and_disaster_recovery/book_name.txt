Primary book: Site Reliability Engineering: How Google Runs Production Systems, 1st edition — Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, and Niall Richard Murphy, editors.
Source: https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/
Source type: Official open book.
Read: Data Integrity and reliable data processing themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define recovery point and time objectives.
02. Compare backups and replication.
03. Verify backup completeness and readability.
04. Restore dependencies in order.
05. Detect silent data corruption.
06. Exercise recovery under a failure model.
Completion: Restore a small service from backup into a clean environment and measure achieved recovery time and data loss.
