Primary book: Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach, 7th edition — John L. Hennessy and David A. Patterson.
Source: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/computer-architecture/hennessy/978-0-443-15406-5
Source type: Publisher book page and contents.
Read: Fundamentals of Quantitative Design and Analysis and Warehouse-Scale Systems themes.
Study checklist:
01. Apply Amdahl's law.
02. Compare latency, throughput, and energy.
03. Separate peak and sustained performance.
04. Explain power and thermal constraints.
05. Evaluate storage and interconnect bottlenecks.
06. State workload-dependent design tradeoffs.
Completion: Compare two hypothetical system designs using workload measurements, an explicit cost model, and sensitivity analysis.
Extension book: CMOS VLSI Design: A Circuits and Systems Perspective, 4th edition — Neil H. E. Weste and David Money Harris.
Extension focus (optional): Investigate the physical implementation costs of an architectural design.
Source: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/cmos-vlsi-design-a-circuits-and-systems-perspective/P200000003427
