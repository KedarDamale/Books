Primary book: Digital Design and Computer Architecture: RISC-V Edition — Sarah L. Harris and David Harris.
Source: https://pages.hmc.edu/harris/ddca/ddcarv.html

Read: Architecture themes: RISC-V instructions, addressing, procedures, and machine encoding.
Study checklist:
01. Distinguish ISA and microarchitecture.
02. Decode registers and immediate fields.
03. Translate loads, stores, and branches.
04. Trace stack-based procedure calls.
05. Explain caller and callee obligations.
06. Read assembly emitted from Rust.
Completion: Compile a small Rust function, annotate its assembly, and trace an input through registers and memory.
