Primary book: Software Foundations: Logical Foundations — Benjamin C. Pierce and collaborators.
Source: https://softwarefoundations.cis.upenn.edu/lf-current/
Source type: Open machine-checked textbook.
Read: Basics, Induction, Lists, Logic, and inductively defined propositions themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define inductive datatypes.
02. State a theorem in a proof assistant.
03. Distinguish computation and proof steps.
04. Choose an induction hypothesis.
05. Prove a list-function property.
06. Identify admitted or unproved assumptions.
Completion: Machine-check a proof about a simple recursive function and state every remaining assumption; proof-language syntax is a local tool, not a new primary language track.
