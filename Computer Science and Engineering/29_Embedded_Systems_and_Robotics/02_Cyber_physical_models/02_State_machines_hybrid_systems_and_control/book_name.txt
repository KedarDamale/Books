Primary book: Introduction to Embedded Systems: A Cyber-Physical Systems Approach, 2nd edition — Edward A. Lee and Sanjit A. Seshia.
Source: https://ptolemy.berkeley.edu/books/leeseshia/
Source type: Author-provided open book.
Read: State Machines, Hybrid Systems, composition, and feedback themes.
Study checklist:
01. Combine discrete modes and continuous dynamics.
02. Define guards and resets.
03. Analyze mode-transition invariants.
04. Explain feedback and stability intuition.
05. Identify sampling and actuation delays.
06. Check reachable unsafe states.
Completion: Model a thermostat or motor controller and demonstrate a safety invariant plus a delay-induced failure.
