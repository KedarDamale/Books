Primary book: Causal Inference: What If — Miguel A. Hernan and James M. Robins.
Source: https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook
Source type: Author-provided open book.
Read: A definition of causal effect, randomized experiments, observational studies, exchangeability, positivity, and consistency themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define an intervention and target effect.
02. Draw a causal DAG.
03. Identify confounders, mediators, and colliders.
04. State exchangeability and positivity assumptions.
05. Separate identification and estimation.
06. Demonstrate bias from conditioning on a collider.
Completion: Simulate a confounded study and compare the observed association with an identified effect under stated assumptions.
