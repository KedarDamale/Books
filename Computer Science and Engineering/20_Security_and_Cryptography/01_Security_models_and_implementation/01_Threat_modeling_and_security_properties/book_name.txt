Primary book: Security Engineering: A Guide to Building Dependable Distributed Systems, 3rd edition — Ross Anderson.
Source: https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/archive/rja14/book.html
Source type: Author-provided open book chapters.
Read: What is Security Engineering, Who is the Opponent, and Boundaries themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define assets and adversary capabilities.
02. Separate confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
03. Draw trust boundaries.
04. Identify abuse cases.
05. Match controls to threats.
06. State residual risk and assumptions.
Completion: Threat-model a small service and demonstrate a concrete attack path prevented by one chosen control.
