Primary book: Site Reliability Engineering: How Google Runs Production Systems, 1st edition — Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, and Niall Richard Murphy, editors.
Source: https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/
Source type: Official open book.
Read: Service Level Objectives, Addressing Cascading Failures, Data Integrity, and Postmortem Culture themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define user-visible AI service objectives.
02. Separate model quality and service reliability.
03. Bound provider timeouts and retries.
04. Preserve human-review state through restarts.
05. Detect stale evidence and incomplete workflows.
06. Write an incident account grounded in traces.
Completion: Run a local failure exercise for a reviewed AI workflow and demonstrate recovery without losing review decisions or duplicating external effects.
