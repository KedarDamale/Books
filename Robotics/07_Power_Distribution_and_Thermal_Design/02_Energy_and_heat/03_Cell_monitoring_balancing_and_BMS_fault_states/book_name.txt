Study checklist:
01. Separate pack and individual-cell measurements.
02. Define chemistry-specific operating limits.
03. Explain passive balancing objectives.
04. Distinguish estimation and protective thresholds.
05. Model open-sensor and overtemperature faults.
06. Define controlled charge and discharge permission states.
Completion: Design a simulated pack-monitor state machine with parameterized manufacturer limits and test undervoltage, overvoltage, sensor faults, and loss of communications.
