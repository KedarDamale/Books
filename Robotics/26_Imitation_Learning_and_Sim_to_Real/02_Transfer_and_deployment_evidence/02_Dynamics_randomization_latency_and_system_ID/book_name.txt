Primary book: Sim-to-Real Transfer of Robotic Control with Dynamics Randomization — Xue Bin Peng, Marcin Andrychowicz, Wojciech Zaremba, and Pieter Abbeel.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06537
Source type: Authors' open research paper.
Read: Randomized physical parameters, policy training, recurrent control, and transfer experiments themes.
Study checklist:
01. Randomize physically plausible model parameters.
02. Include sensing and actuation delay.
03. Distinguish episode-constant and rapidly changing variation.
04. Calibrate distributions from measurements.
05. Compare nominal and randomized policies.
06. Identify simulator exploitation.
Completion: Train or evaluate a simulated policy across a documented parameter envelope and test failures just outside that envelope.
