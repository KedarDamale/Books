Primary book: NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev 2 — NASA systems engineering contributors.
Source: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/
Source type: Official open engineering handbook.
Read: Model analysis, verification, validation, and technical risk themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define the purpose of the simulator.
02. Identify parameters relevant to that purpose.
03. Compare models with measured responses.
04. Validate contact and actuator behavior separately.
05. Quantify unvalidated assumptions.
06. Decide what evidence requires physical testing.
Completion: Write a simulator-validation report using held-out measurements or synthetic ground truth and identify unsupported deployment claims.
