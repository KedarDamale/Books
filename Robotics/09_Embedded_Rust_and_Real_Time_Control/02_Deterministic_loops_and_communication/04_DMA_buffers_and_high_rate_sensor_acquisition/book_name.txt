01. Separate CPU-driven and DMA-driven transfers.
02. Define buffer ownership across interrupts.
03. Detect overruns and partial buffers.
04. Tie samples to acquisition timing.
05. Account for bus and memory contention.
06. Bound data age before control use.
Completion: Model or implement double-buffered sensor acquisition in Rust and test buffer rollover, delayed processing, and overrun detection.
