Primary book: Control Barrier Functions: Theory and Applications — Aaron D. Ames, Samuel Coogan, Magnus Egerstedt, Gennaro Notomista, Koushil Sreenath, and Paulo Tabuada.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11199
Source type: Authors' open research paper.
Read: Forward invariance, barrier conditions, and quadratic-program control filters themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define a constraint set mathematically.
02. Derive a simple barrier inequality.
03. Formulate a minimally modifying action filter.
04. State model and relative-degree assumptions.
05. Handle actuator limits and infeasibility.
06. Distinguish model-based invariance and real-system evidence.
Completion: Implement a barrier-filtered simulated controller and test feasible, infeasible, delayed, and model-mismatched cases.
