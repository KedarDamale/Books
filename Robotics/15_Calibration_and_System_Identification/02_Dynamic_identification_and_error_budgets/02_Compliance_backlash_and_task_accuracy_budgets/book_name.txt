Primary book: Precision Machine Design, MIT 2.75 course materials — Martin Culpepper and Alexander Slocum.
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-75-precision-machine-design-fall-2001/
Source type: Open university course materials.
Read: Error budgets, stiffness, compliance, and precision-design themes.
Study checklist:
01. Measure direction-dependent positioning error.
02. Separate geometric and elastic error.
03. Relate load and deflection.
04. Quantify backlash dead zones.
05. Combine independent and correlated error sources carefully.
06. Prioritize improvements by task sensitivity.
Completion: Construct an endpoint-accuracy budget and show whether improved sensing, mechanics, or calibration offers the largest benefit.
