Primary book: OpenCV Tutorials and Camera Calibration and 3D Reconstruction reference — OpenCV contributors.
Source: https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/df8/tutorial_root.html
Source type: Official online documentation.
Read: Hand-eye calibration, robot-world hand-eye calibration, pose estimation, and homogeneous transforms themes.
Study checklist:
01. Distinguish eye-in-hand and fixed-camera layouts.
02. Construct consistent transform equations.
03. Collect sufficiently varied poses.
04. Estimate camera-to-robot transforms.
05. Calibrate a tool-center offset.
06. Propagate calibration errors into grasp position.
Completion: Solve a synthetic hand-eye problem and quantify how limited motion diversity or mixed transform conventions corrupt the result.
Required second source: Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control — Kevin M. Lynch and Frank C. Park.
Read: Rigid transforms, frame composition, and robot kinematics. Required for consistent robot-camera-tool equations.
Source: https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/
