Primary book: NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods: Measurement Process Characterization — NIST and SEMATECH contributors.
Source: https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/mpc.htm
Source type: Official open engineering handbook.
Read: Calibration, gauge studies, bias, stability, and uncertainty themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define a joint reference convention.
02. Estimate scale and zero offset.
03. Separate accuracy and repeatability.
04. Repeat homing from different directions.
05. Quantify thermal and setup variation.
06. Preserve calibration version and validity.
Completion: Design a joint calibration study and report offset, repeatability, uncertainty, and conditions under which the result applies.
