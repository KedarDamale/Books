01. Inject stale and missing sensor data.
02. Corrupt or delay command messages.
03. Simulate jams and unexpected contact.
04. Exercise startup and shutdown failures.
05. Verify invariants through all modes.
06. Preserve a reproducible regression record.
Completion: Build a fault-injection suite for a simulated or hardware-in-the-loop joint and report violations with exact state and command traces.
