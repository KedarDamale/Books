Primary book: Robotic Manipulation: Perception, Planning, and Control — Russ Tedrake.
Source: https://manipulation.mit.edu/
Source type: Open MIT course text.
Read: Arms, Hands, simple grippers, and soft or underactuated hands themes.
Study checklist:
01. Compare rigid and compliant actuation.
02. Explain series elasticity and force estimation.
03. Relate tendon routing and friction.
04. Analyze underactuated finger motion.
05. Distinguish grip-force and aperture control.
06. Identify payload-retention failure modes.
Completion: Compare two gripper actuation mechanisms and model the effect of friction, compliance, and an unexpected object contact.
