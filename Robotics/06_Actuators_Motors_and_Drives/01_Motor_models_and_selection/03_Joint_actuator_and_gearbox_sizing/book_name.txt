Primary book: Electric Motors and Drives: Fundamentals, Types and Applications, 5th edition — Austin Hughes and Bill Drury.
Source: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/electric-motors-and-drives/hughes/978-0-08-102615-1
Source type: Publisher book page and contents.
Read: Motor selection, drive ratings, thermal behavior, and servo-system themes.
Study checklist:
01. Compute gravity and acceleration torque.
02. Apply transmission efficiency consistently.
03. Reflect load inertia to the motor.
04. Calculate RMS and peak demand.
05. Compare required and available speed.
06. Analyze overload and duty-cycle margins.
Completion: Size a shoulder or elbow actuator from a measured or simulated trajectory and show why a stall-torque-only choice is inadequate.
