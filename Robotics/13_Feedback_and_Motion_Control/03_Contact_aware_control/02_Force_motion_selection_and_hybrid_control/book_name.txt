Primary book: Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control — Kevin M. Lynch and Frank C. Park.
Source: https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/
Source type: Official book resources and author materials.
Read: Force Control and Hybrid Motion-Force Control themes.
Study checklist:
01. Separate constrained and free motion directions.
02. Choose force and position objectives.
03. Define a contact frame.
04. Transform selection directions correctly.
05. Manage contact-mode transitions.
06. Identify inconsistent simultaneous objectives.
Completion: Design a surface-following experiment in simulation with force regulation normal to the surface and motion along it.

Extension focus (optional): Continue to nonlinear stability and optimization-based control.
