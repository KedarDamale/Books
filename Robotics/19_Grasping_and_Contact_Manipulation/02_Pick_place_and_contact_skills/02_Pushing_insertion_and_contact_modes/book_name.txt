Primary book: Robotic Manipulation: Perception, Planning, and Control — Russ Tedrake.
Source: https://manipulation.mit.edu/
Source type: Open MIT course text.
Read: Mobile manipulation, contact-rich manipulation, and force-control themes.
Study checklist:
01. Model deliberate contact as task state.
02. Distinguish sticking and sliding motion.
03. Use compliance during constrained motion.
04. Identify jamming and wedging conditions.
05. Compare open-loop and feedback execution.
06. Define disengagement and recovery behavior.
Completion: Simulate pushing or a simple insertion task and compare rigid position control with a contact-aware compliant strategy.
