Primary book: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design — Richard G. Budynas and J. Keith Nisbett.
Source: https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/shigleys-mechanical-engineering-design-nisbett.html
Source type: Publisher book page; obtain a legitimate edition.
Read: Mechanical springs, clutches, brakes, and static loading themes.
Study checklist:
01. Calculate gravity torque over the workspace.
02. Model a spring counterbalance.
03. Distinguish holding and dynamic braking.
04. Analyze a power-loss load path.
05. Compare counterweight and spring tradeoffs.
06. Identify failure modes in a holding mechanism.
Completion: Model an arm's worst-case gravity load and compare a counterbalance and a holding-brake strategy including loss of drive power.
