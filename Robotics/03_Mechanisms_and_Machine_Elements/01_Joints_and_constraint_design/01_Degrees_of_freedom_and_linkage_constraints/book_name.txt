Study checklist:
01. Count unconstrained motion dimensions.
02. Classify revolute and prismatic joints.
03. Apply a mobility count with assumptions.
04. Identify redundant constraints.
05. Distinguish local and global mobility.
06. Construct a mechanism with a singular configuration.
Completion: Analyze the mobility of three candidate arm or gripper mechanisms and explain a case where naive constraint counting misleads.
