Primary book: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design — Richard G. Budynas and J. Keith Nisbett.
Source: https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/shigleys-mechanical-engineering-design-nisbett.html
Source type: Publisher book page; obtain a legitimate edition.
Read: Static failure, stress concentration, and column-loading themes.
Study checklist:
01. Distinguish yielding and instability.
02. Identify stress raisers at holes and shoulders.
03. Choose a relevant failure criterion.
04. Estimate a buckling load.
05. Define a margin against a stated load case.
06. Explain why a single safety factor is insufficient evidence.
Completion: Analyze a loaded bracket or slender member and identify which failure mode governs under several mounting assumptions.
