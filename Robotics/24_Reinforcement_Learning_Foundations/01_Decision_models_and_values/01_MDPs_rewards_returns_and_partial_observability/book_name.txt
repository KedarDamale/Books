Study checklist:
01. Define states, actions, rewards, and transitions.
02. Explain discounted and episodic returns.
03. State the Markov assumption.
04. Distinguish hidden state and observation.
05. Separate reward and intended task success.
06. Compare stochastic and deterministic policies.
Completion: Formulate a reaching or object-moving task as an MDP and show a state omission that makes the chosen observation non-Markov.
