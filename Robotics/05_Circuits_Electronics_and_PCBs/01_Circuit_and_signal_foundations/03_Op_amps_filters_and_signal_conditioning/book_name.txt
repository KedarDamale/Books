Primary book: Foundations of Analog and Digital Electronic Circuits — Anant Agarwal and Jeffrey H. Lang.
Source: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/pages/readings/
Source type: Book with official MIT reading guide.
Read: Amplifiers, op-amp abstraction, feedback, and filter themes.
Study checklist:
01. State ideal amplifier assumptions.
02. Calculate gain and input range.
03. Explain common-mode and output-swing limits.
04. Design a simple low-pass interface.
05. Compare noise gain and signal gain.
06. Identify saturation and bandwidth failures.
Completion: Model a sensor amplifier and filter and demonstrate a case where supply range or bandwidth invalidates the ideal calculation.
