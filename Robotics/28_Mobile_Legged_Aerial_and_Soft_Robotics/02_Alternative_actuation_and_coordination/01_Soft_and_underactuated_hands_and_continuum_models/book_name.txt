Primary book: Robotic Manipulation: Perception, Planning, and Control — Russ Tedrake.
Source: https://manipulation.mit.edu/
Source type: Open MIT course text.
Read: Hands, soft or underactuated end effectors, compliance, and contact themes.
Study checklist:
01. Compare rigid-link and distributed deformation models.
02. Explain passive adaptation to object shape.
03. Identify hysteresis and material uncertainty.
04. Choose sensing for an underactuated mechanism.
05. Relate compliance and grasp robustness.
06. State where lumped models fail.
Completion: Model a simple compliant or underactuated gripper and compare its contact adaptation with a rigid-finger design.
