Primary book: Hydraulics and Pneumatics: A Technician's and Engineer's Guide, 3rd edition — Andrew Parr.
Source: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/hydraulics-and-pneumatics/parr/978-0-08-096674-8
Source type: Publisher book page and contents.
Read: Hydraulic actuators, pumps, proportional control, load holding, and circuit safety themes.
Study checklist:
01. Relate pressure, flow, force, and speed.
02. Compare hydraulic and pneumatic stiffness.
03. Explain relief and load-holding functions.
04. Account for fluid heating and losses.
05. Identify leakage and contamination effects.
06. Model loss of pressure or hose integrity.
Completion: Analyze a hydraulic-axis concept and document its force-speed envelope, load path, thermal losses, and controlled response to pressure loss.
