Primary book: Finite Element Analysis, MIT mechanics-of-materials module (2001) — David Roylance.
Source: https://web.mit.edu/course/3/3.11/www/modules/fea.pdf
Source type: Author-provided university teaching text.
Read: Element stiffness, assembly, boundary conditions, stress calculations, and FEA limitations themes.
Study checklist:
01. Construct a simple stiffness element.
02. Assemble a small structural system.
03. Apply physically justified supports and loads.
04. Distinguish stress concentration and numerical singularity.
05. Check mesh and model sensitivity.
06. Compare with hand calculation or measurement.
Completion: Analyze a link or bracket using a small Python model or CAD solver and present convergence, boundary-condition sensitivity, and an independent sanity check.
