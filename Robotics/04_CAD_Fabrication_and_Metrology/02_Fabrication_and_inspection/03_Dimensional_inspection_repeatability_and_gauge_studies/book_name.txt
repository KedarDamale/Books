Primary book: NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods: Measurement Process Characterization — NIST and SEMATECH contributors.
Source: https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/mpc.htm
Source type: Official open engineering handbook.
Read: Gauge studies, calibration, repeatability, reproducibility, and uncertainty themes.
Study checklist:
01. Choose an instrument for a dimensional requirement.
02. Separate part variation and gauge variation.
03. Design repeated measurements.
04. Check instrument bias and stability.
05. Quantify operator and setup effects.
06. Report uncertainty against a tolerance.
Completion: Perform or simulate a gauge study for a joint dimension and decide whether the measurement process resolves the required tolerance.
