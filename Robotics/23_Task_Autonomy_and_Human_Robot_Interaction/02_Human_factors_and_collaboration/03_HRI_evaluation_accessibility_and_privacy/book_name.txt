Primary book: Human-Robot Interaction: An Introduction, 2nd edition — Christoph Bartneck, Tony Belpaeme, Friederike Eyssel, Takayuki Kanda, Merel Keijsers, and Selma Sabanovic.
Source: https://www.human-robot-interaction.org/
Source type: Authors' official site with open book.
Read: Research methods, evaluation, ethics, and social context themes.
Study checklist:
01. Define measurable interaction outcomes.
02. Compare task success and user burden.
03. Identify accessibility requirements.
04. Protect recorded images and interaction data.
05. Separate small-study observations and broad conclusions.
06. Document stakeholder and sampling limitations.
Completion: Design a small study for an operator interface with task metrics, failure scenarios, accessibility checks, and data-handling rules.
