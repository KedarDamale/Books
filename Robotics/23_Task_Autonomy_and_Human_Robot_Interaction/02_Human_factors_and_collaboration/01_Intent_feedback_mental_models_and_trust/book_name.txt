Primary book: Human-Robot Interaction: An Introduction, 2nd edition — Christoph Bartneck, Tony Belpaeme, Friederike Eyssel, Takayuki Kanda, Merel Keijsers, and Selma Sabanovic.
Source: https://www.human-robot-interaction.org/
Source type: Authors' official site with open book.
Read: Interaction design, communication, trust, and human expectations themes.
Study checklist:
01. Show the robot's current mode and intended action.
02. Distinguish success feedback and progress indication.
03. Explain uncertainty in user-understandable terms.
04. Avoid overstating robot understanding.
05. Support interruption and correction.
06. Evaluate user expectations against actual behavior.
Completion: Prototype an operator interaction and evaluate whether users can predict motion, identify faults, and stop or correct a task.
